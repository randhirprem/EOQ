A little math. A lot of clarity.

Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) finds the order size that minimizes the cost of replenishing and storing your inventory.

The formula behind the balance

EOQ = √(2 × D × S) / H

Keep in mind

EOQ assumes steady demand, constant costs, and instant replenishment. Purchase costs and stockout costs are not included.

Interpret the whole model

Choose Linear Regression, ARIMA or seasonal SARIMA. Missing months count as zero; confirm that missing deliveries really mean zero demand.

Inspect R², adjusted R², coefficient tests and residual ACF/PACF together. A small model p-value is not proof of forecast accuracy. Different fitting windows are not directly comparable.
